# Additional recovered trading / crypto experiments

This directory is part of the historical `cb` recovery set. It adds crypto market analysis,
ATH tracking, exchange experiments, Robinhood/RSI tests, yield-curve analysis, news crawling,
and small utilities recovered from a later folder batch.

Cleanup intentionally removed:
- hard-coded exchange/login credentials and account identifiers
- duplicate backup copies and exact duplicate files
- generated CSV/ODS datasets
- credential-only notes
- vendored or generated artifacts

Some scripts depend on APIs or third-party libraries as they existed around 2019–2021 and are
preserved primarily as historical source.
